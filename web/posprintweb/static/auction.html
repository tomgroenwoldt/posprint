Up for auction

Frames of receipts sent to this printer by strangers. No theme and no editing — whatever turned up. Bidding happens on eBay.

The bidding happens on eBay, not here — their pages refuse to be embedded in anyone else's site, so the buttons above open a new tab. Every picture here is a photograph of the actual object.

The printer is still running. Send it something and it comes out on paper in my flat; the good ones end up in the gallery, and one day probably in another frame.
